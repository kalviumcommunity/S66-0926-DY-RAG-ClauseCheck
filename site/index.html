How an answer is checked

Each check runs against one contract and its amendments only. These are the steps, and what you will see at each.

  1. Find the clauses. The question is matched to clauses in that contract and its amendments, and the current version of each term is chosen.Found 2 clauses in 2 Orion documents · 0.4 s
  2. Write from those clauses only. The answer may use nothing else, and says so when the contracts do not contain the term.Writing the answer from those clauses only
  3. Check every citation and number. The program, not the model, looks for each cited clause and each figure in the document text.2 of 2 citations found in the documents
    3 of 3 numbers match the clause text
  4. Show the clause. The cited page is shown with the exact words the answer relies on highlighted, so you can read the source.Checked · or · Verify, naming the number that was not found

What it will not do

The limits, stated up front.

  • No questions across contracts. A check covers one contract. A question that names another contract is refused, not answered from the wrong one.
  • No guessing. If the contract has no such term, the answer is “No term found”, and nothing is invented to fill the gap.
  • No estimating redacted values. A figure hidden in a filing stays hidden.
  • No legal advice. Answers quote contracts. They do not say what you should do.
